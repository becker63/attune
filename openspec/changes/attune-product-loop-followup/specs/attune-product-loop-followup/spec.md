## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Full Attune product loop is scoped after ARS

The full Attune Discovery product loop SHALL be planned as a follow-up to the
ARS substrate migration.

#### Scenario: Product loop work is implemented
- **WHEN** recall, proof, decision, report, or promotion behavior is added
- **THEN** it is expressed through Recipes or ManagedRecipes
- **AND** validation evidence is recorded through receipts, diagnostics,
  repairs, and health projections
- **AND** legacy SQLite/Drizzle/PgTyped or generated-companion compatibility
  lanes are not reintroduced.
