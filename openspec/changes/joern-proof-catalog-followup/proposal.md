# Joern Proof Catalog Follow-Up

## Why

ARS migrates code generation and proof-routing surfaces to Recipes, but it does
not finish the full Joern proof catalog or long proof campaigns.

## What Changes

- Define the proof catalog, template families, routing rules, and campaign
  budgets.
- Express generated bindings, proof templates, and campaign runs as Recipes or
  ManagedRecipes.
- Record proof observations as normalized receipt evidence.

## Non-Goals

- Do not make ARS responsible for all proof templates or long campaigns.
- Do not allow arbitrary agent-authored Joern queries as the normal path.
