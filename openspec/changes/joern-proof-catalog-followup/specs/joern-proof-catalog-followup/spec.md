## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Joern proof catalog is recipe-backed follow-up work

The full Joern proof catalog and long proof campaigns SHALL be planned outside
ARS.

#### Scenario: Proof catalog work is implemented
- **WHEN** Joern bindings, proof templates, routing rules, or campaigns are
  added
- **THEN** they are expressed as Recipes or ManagedRecipes
- **AND** observations are normalized into receipt evidence
- **AND** arbitrary agent-authored Joern queries are not the default workflow.
