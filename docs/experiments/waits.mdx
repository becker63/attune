Extraction averaged 344.58 seconds and reached 2,548.744 seconds, or 42.5 minutes. The long tail motivates reusing exact acquisition and scheduling independent work.

These are separate runs, not a simultaneous timeline. Dividing Jev's prepared-request median into Sol's whole-turn median does not measure an end-to-end speedup. Their 88-task timing populations include three tasks outside the accuracy cohort.

A separate partly cached, interrupted build had 180 process intervals totaling 9.34 hours in a roughly 5.03-hour span, with up to seven overlapping. It was not a successful cold full-corpus run.

Embedding acquisition had 27,733 logical rows but 10,555 distinct prepared inputs, acquired with 351 requests. Unchanged warm replay made no new provider calls; warm local wall time was unavailable.
