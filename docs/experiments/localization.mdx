The original panel had 96 tasks in 24 repository groups. Runtime-limited exclusions left 88 tasks in 22 groups; 85 had eligible gold, containing 218 files. Eligible splits were development 50, calibration 20, and final 15. The small final split limits generalization.

Gold contains existing modified/deleted JS/TS paths, excluding new files and tests. Prediction never reads it. Ranking arms share a frozen 29,493-candidate universe.

| Condition | Hit@1 | Hit@10 | Recall@10 | All files @10 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Lexical/path prior | 11.8% | 35.3% | 23.4% | 16.5% |
| Prior + anchors | 18.8% | 41.2% | 27.1% | 20.0% |
| + admitted facts | 16.5% | 41.2% | 28.0% | 21.2% |
| + bounded Theory | 12.9% | 41.2% | 27.2% | 21.2% |
| Hosted Qwen retrieval | 41.2% | 85.9% | 64.0% | 44.7% |
| Qwen + Jev | 57.6% | 90.6% | 72.0% | 52.9% |
| Qwen + structure + Jev | 61.2% | 91.8% | 73.3% | 55.3% |

All rows are task-micro on 85 eligible tasks. Deterministic arms are cumulative. Hit finds any required file; Recall averages recovered fractions; complete-file accuracy requires every required file.

## What changed the direction

Anchors helped the lexical prior. More additive structure reduced top-one accuracy. Equal top-ten aggregates concealed gains and losses. Hosted judgment used structure differently, as context for decisions over retrieved candidates, and improved these cohort results.

The inspected Sol arm had 60/85 top-one hits but used a different answer protocol; its 20 read-floor failures stayed misses. It is not a ranking comparison under identical procedures.

## Costs and repeated judgments

Qwen acquisition reported $0.1409903; structural Jev judgment $0.023915388. Together they were about 28.2× below a $4.6544292 API-equivalent estimate for recorded Sol inspection. Sol actually ran on a subscription; its plan bill is unknown. Compute is excluded and quality was not matched.

Five observations of the lexical judgments had modest top-ten effects and did not establish free improvement from larger answers. The report retains unions and equal-size controls. [Cost receipts](https://atlas-live-five.vercel.app/costs.json).
