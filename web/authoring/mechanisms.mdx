### Give equivalent questions the same identity

Role numbers are placeholders. Consistently renaming them must not create a new structural question. Canonicalization sorts, removes duplicates and renumbers the description before computing its identity.

<FlixBlock name="canonical" />

```text
<same relations, renamed slots>
                |
                v
           canonical
                |
                v
       <one descriptor identity>
```
