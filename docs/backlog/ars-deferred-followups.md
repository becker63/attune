# ARS Deferred Follow-Ups

ARS keeps these as backlog intent, not active OpenSpec changes, while the
clean-fork topology and substrate migration settle.

## Attune Product Loop

- Define the full recall-to-proof-to-decision-to-report product loop after the
  Recipe/ManagedRecipe, TimescaleDB/Postgres receipt, and Tend/OpenCode control
  substrate is stable.
- Promote behavior only when it can be expressed through Recipes or
  ManagedRecipes with receipts, diagnostics, repairs, and validation evidence.
- Do not re-open SQLite/Drizzle/PgTyped, generated companion, or artifact
  ownership compatibility lanes.

## Canopy Live Deployment

- Scope live deployment, rollout, rollback, readiness, destroy, and drift
  repair with human review gates.
- Express platform resources as ManagedRecipes with Effect Alchemy lifecycle.
- Keep Nix, Arion, nix2container, scheduler/admission, provider, and
  destructive infrastructure work behind explicit review.

## FoldKit Product Surface

- Define the full FoldKit workbench surface after durable recipe receipts,
  health, diagnostics, decisions, and product projections are available.
- Keep durable truth in the shared recipe receipt spine rather than private UI
  ledgers.
- Validate UI/product behavior separately from ARS substrate cleanup.

## Joern Proof Catalog

- Define proof catalog, template families, routing rules, campaign budgets, and
  observation normalization after ARS recipe-shaped generation is stable.
- Express generated bindings, proof templates, and campaign runs as Recipes or
  ManagedRecipes.
- Do not allow arbitrary agent-authored Joern queries as the default path.
