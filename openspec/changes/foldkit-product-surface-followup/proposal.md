# FoldKit Product Surface Follow-Up

## Why

ARS keeps FoldKit product/UI completion out of scope. The full workbench
surface needs its own product and validation plan after the recipe substrate is
available.

## What Changes

- Define the full FoldKit workbench product surface.
- Connect UI state to recipe receipts, health, diagnostics, decisions, and
  product projections.
- Keep durable truth in the substrate instead of private UI ledgers.

## Non-Goals

- Do not make ARS responsible for full UI/product behavior.
- Do not bypass the shared recipe receipt spine for durable discovery truth.
