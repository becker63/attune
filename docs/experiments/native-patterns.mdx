title: "Native Datalog patterns"
description: "Useful concrete recoveries did not produce a better general selector."

The October 13 campaign froze 64 native patterns from 113 candidates and 595 ranking configurations. It reused the earlier localization panel and Qwen acquisition; no new embedding or Jev calls ran.

Forty-seven eligible tasks in 14 groups completed structural execution. The primary population retained all 85 eligible tasks in 22 groups, scoring unavailable structural execution as misses. Complete-case results answer a narrower question.

| Completed-subset treatment | Repository-macro Recall@10 | MRR@10 | All files @10 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Qwen | 62.424% | 65.238% | 43.452% |
| Existing Studio fusion | 73.810% | 62.689% | 54.762% |
| Import/call agreement expansion | 67.781% | 50.357% | 48.810% |
| Direct-import fixed-pool reranking | 67.767% | 64.404% | 47.024% |

Agreement added five gold files outside Qwen's first 32 without displacing gold in that completed subset. Individual recoveries were real, but the established Studio recipe remained stronger on Recall@10. Some richer patterns were harmful or returned baseline unchanged.

Every group-held-out selection fold chose Identity, exactly reproducing Qwen. Full-cohort repository-macro Recall@10 was 63.8909%, with no selection gain. Hundreds of correlated configurations are not hundreds of independent observations.

Fresh-client payload reconstruction, independent recomputation, scratch rollback, nominal typed fixtures, and ranking parity tested execution correctness separately from utility. Four extraction timeouts remained preserved.
