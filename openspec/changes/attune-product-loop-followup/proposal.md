# Attune Product Loop Follow-Up

## Why

ARS intentionally stops at the substrate migration. The full Attune Discovery
product loop still needs its own scoped plan after Recipe/ManagedRecipe,
TimescaleDB/Postgres receipts, and Tend/OpenCode control are established.

## What Changes

- Define the full recall-to-proof-to-decision-to-report product loop.
- Promote only behavior that can be expressed through Recipes or
  ManagedRecipes with receipts, diagnostics, repairs, and validation evidence.
- Keep ARS substrate decisions as prerequisites rather than re-opening
  SQLite/Drizzle/PgTyped or generated-companion compatibility lanes.

## Non-Goals

- Do not change ARS scope.
- Do not reintroduce legacy substrate compatibility.
