title: "The story of Attune"
description: "The experiments that changed what the project could credibly claim."
---

Attune asks whether small programs over repository structure can reduce repeated work in software agents. The development record supports several answers, with different strengths.

## First: locate the expensive work

The [five-wait study](/experiments/waits) separated embedding requests, extraction, Theory preparation, prepared judgment, and whole inspection turns. Extraction had a long tail. This made acquisition and scheduling promising targets, but unequal timer boundaries prevented a whole-agent speedup claim.

The [historical scheduling experiment](/experiments/scheduling) showed exact offline work could be scheduled remotely and replayed cheaply. It supplied execution evidence for the reuse idea.

## Then: test whether structure improves answers

[Localization](/experiments/localization) compared lexical, embedding, and judged retrieval. Anchors helped. Richer additive structural scores regressed at top one. Prepared structural context helped hosted reranking on the retained cohort. That shifted the question toward how structure is used, rather than whether more facts are always better.

[ReviewBench](/experiments/reviewbench) tested the economic implication in review. Its optional packet treatment failed to land: all three calls were refused. Quality corrections mattered, but no review-efficiency gain was demonstrated.

[Coordination](/experiments/coordination) implemented witness-bearing plans without completing the original real panel. [Repository-through-time](/experiments/repository-history) found useful structural signals and important blind spots.

## Finally: separate concrete mechanisms from attractive generalizations

[Native patterns](/experiments/native-patterns) exposed real helper recoveries and substantial graph unavailability. [Specialization](/experiments/specialization) tested repository-specific policies; it did not beat the fixed Studio default. [Agreement recovery](/experiments/agreement) explained striking React gains, found false nominal joins elsewhere, and isolated promising resolver repairs.

Meanwhile [Studio extraction](/experiments/cold-extraction) supplied controlled evidence for cheaper uncached work without changing the source population or Flix admission.

## Where that leaves the project

The most defensible contribution is an inspectable boundary between semantics and costly acquisition, with exact replay and explicit uncertainty. Retrieval results remain exploratory, review efficiency remains unproven, and structural signatures remain instruments rather than quality scores.

Active bounded-search code is listed in the [research reference](/reference/research). Its presence is not a completed result. Every chapter identifies the population and record supporting its claims.
