The recovery study reproduced React's 62.5% → 100% result. Two missing helpers were reachable from Qwen anchors through same-anchor imports and owned-call relationships. One helper began at rank 208, requiring expansion; the other began at rank 22, where reranking sufficed. Both gains survived a separate conservative source-binding qualification.

That concrete mechanism did not validate a broad new policy default. The full operational account retained 85 issues in 22 groups, with 38 explicit fallbacks.

| Procedure | Repository-macro Recall@10 |
| --- | ---: |
| Qwen | 63.8909% |
| Fixed Studio with fallback | 70.0004% |
| Expanded primary shrinkage selector | 67.1325% |
| Fixed Studio with exact-path admission repair | 71.1368% |
| Matched five-choice selector, original facts | 68.5311% |
| Matched five-choice selector, repaired facts | 72.1295% |

Matched admission comparisons preserve the candidate bank. Comparing a repaired small bank with a larger original selector would confound two changes.

## Why soundness matters

Agreement filtered many call-only distractors. Source inspection also found unsupported nominal joins: matching callee names did not always establish correct lexical binding. A source-qualified arm narrows the claim and leaves namespace, receiver, parameter, and other unresolved flows unknown.

The results favor concrete admission and binding improvements over assuming more anchors or patterns are better. The cohort was already exposed and repository samples were small; no untouched replication or whole-agent speedup was established.

The study executed native predictions, fitting, application, and evaluation; label mutations reused identical prediction actions. Selected-only join timings exclude extraction and include fresh-JVM/JIT effects.
