## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: FoldKit product surface follows ARS substrate

The full FoldKit workbench product/UI SHALL be planned as a follow-up to ARS.

#### Scenario: Workbench behavior is implemented
- **WHEN** FoldKit scene, interaction, report, or progress UI behavior is added
- **THEN** durable truth comes from recipe receipts, health, diagnostics, and
  product projections
- **AND** UI-local state does not become a parallel durable ontology
- **AND** validation covers the user-facing workflow slice.
