---
title: "Coverage and failure"
description: "Keep an empty answer distinct from unavailable knowledge."
---

A complete query with no matches supports an empty answer. Unknown coverage does not support that conclusion. A failed extraction is a failed observation, not a successfully empty graph.

`Coverage` carries these distinctions through query outcomes and witnesses. A structural explanation must identify supported facts in the requested world. Refusal and unresolved references remain visible.

Repository admission checks the source envelope, ranges, definitions, and supported resolution rules before creating typed edges. Syntax extraction is broader than trustworthy semantic admission: a nominal callee name can match a symbol without proving lexical binding or receiver flow.

The [agreement experiment](/experiments/agreement) makes this limitation concrete. Import/call conjunction recovered real helpers, but source inspection also exposed false nominal joins. Its conservative binding-qualified arm has narrower coverage, with other forms left unknown.

Evaluation preserves failures as misses when the task is eligible. Tasks with no eligible gold have undefined metrics. Complete-case diagnostics must state their smaller population and the possibility of nonrandom missingness.
